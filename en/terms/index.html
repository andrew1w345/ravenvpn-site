Raven VPN terms and rules

Basic service terms and acceptable use rules.

Personal use

Raven VPN is intended for personal use. Sharing access keys with third parties is prohibited.

Plans and limits

Traffic, speed and device limits depend on the selected plan. If limits are exceeded, the service can restrict access under product rules.

Renewal

An active subscription can be renewed. If the subscription is still active, renewal is added to the current end date. Details are shown in the bot.

Prohibited use

  • sharing keys with third parties;
  • using the service for spam, DDoS or malicious activity;
  • trying to bypass plan or device limits;
  • using the service for illegal activity.

Access restrictions

If the rules are violated, access can be limited or blocked. For disputed situations, contact support.
