FAQ

Before you start

Short answers about setup, payments and supported apps.

How do I get Raven VPN access?

Open @TheRavenVPNBot, choose a plan and pay. Open Devices and add your device, then import its link or scan its QR code in a compatible VPN app. Each device needs its own link.

Which apps can I use?

Raven VPN works with common VLESS clients such as Hiddify, Happ, v2rayNG, V2Box, Shadowrocket and v2rayN depending on your device.

Can I test it first?

Yes. A 1-day trial costs 1.49 USDT and includes 10 GB, speeds up to 25 Mbps and 2 devices. Available once per Telegram account.

Which payment methods are available?

You can pay with Telegram Stars or CryptoBot. Plan prices are shown in USDT, and the exact amount is displayed before payment.

How does Raven VPN protect my connection?

Raven VPN encrypts the connection between your device and our servers, helping protect your traffic on public and untrusted networks.
