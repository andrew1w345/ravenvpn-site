Raven VPN privacy

What data is needed for service operation, payments and support.

Service data

We store your Telegram account identifier, subscription and device records, payment records and traffic counters to provide and manage VPN access.

Payments

Payment can go through Telegram Stars and CryptoBot. Payment providers process payments under their own rules. Do not send full sensitive payment data to support.

Connection data

We do not analyze the contents of your traffic. VPN servers process connection IP addresses, destination addresses, connection times and traffic volumes. Technical connection logs and activity summaries are used for diagnostics, traffic accounting and detecting copied device links.

Website analytics

This website uses Umami to measure page visits and interactions with links and buttons.

Support

If you message support, your message can be used to resolve the request. Do not send the full connection link, secrets or sensitive data.
