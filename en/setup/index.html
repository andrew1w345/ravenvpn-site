Devices

Choose an app for your platform

The bot sends a connection link and a QR code. Import them into a compatible VPN client.

Android

Hiddify, Happ or v2rayNG. In most cases, paste the subscription link from the bot.

iPhone and iPad

Hiddify, Happ, V2Box or Shadowrocket. You can use either the QR code or the link.

Windows

Hiddify, Happ or v2rayN. After import, choose the added profile and connect.

macOS

Hiddify, Happ or v2rayN. If the link does not import, update the client to the latest version.

If it does not connect

Switch networks, restart the app and check that the Raven VPN profile is selected.

What to send to support

Device, app, error screenshot and approximate time of the issue. Do not send the full connection link.

Basic steps

The same logic for every device

1. Get the link

Open paid access in the bot and copy the subscription URL or open the QR code.

2. Import it

In the app, choose subscription import by link or QR code scan.

3. Connect

Select the added profile, turn on the VPN and check that traffic goes through the client.

Start in Telegram

Choose a plan, pay and receive the connection link with a QR code in the bot.
