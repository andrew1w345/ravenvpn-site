FAQ

Payment questions

Can I buy extra traffic?

Yes. Extra traffic packages +250 GB, +500 GB and +1000 GB are available; they work until the next monthly traffic reset.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Yes. A mid-period plan upgrade is supported. The extra payment is calculated for the remaining time, and the new monthly traffic and speed limits apply immediately.

What should I do if I was charged twice?

Contact support with the payment method, approximate payment time and a screenshot with payment credentials hidden.
